I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at KAIST Interaction Lab (KIXLAB) with Prof. Juho Kim. I hold a Ph.D. in HCI from HKUST, with prior research experience at UChicago Knowledge Lab and MSRA HK. My background as a professional cinematographer shapes my perspective on visual storytelling, spatial framing, and creative control.

My research bridges Visual Analytics, Human-Centered AI (HCAI), and Extended Reality (XR), exploring the "human–AI loop" to enhance human agency across two pillars:

**Immersive Environments for Skill Training**: Interactive systems for expert learning and skill acquisition.

**Human–AI Collaboration**: Multi-agent systems for seamless human–AI co-creation.

My work is published in CHI, UIST, TheWebConf, TVCG, and IEEE VR (Best Paper Honorable Mentions at IEEE VR and ICSC), backed by multiple grants and US/CN patents. I aim to build AI systems that make complex information intuitive while enabling responsible artistic expression.
