I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the KAIST Interaction Lab (KIXLAB), working with Prof. Juho Kim. I received my Ph.D. in Human-Computer Interaction from HKUST and have previously conducted research at the University of Chicago Knowledge Lab and Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA). My background as a professional cinematographer shapes my perspective on visual storytelling, spatial framing, and creative control.

My research bridges **Visual Analytics**, **Human-Centered AI (HCAI)**, and **Extended Reality (XR)**, exploring the human–AI loop to enhance human agency across two primary directions:

**Immersive Environments for Skill Training:** Interactive and adaptive systems that support expert learning, skill acquisition, and embodied interaction.

**Human–AI Collaboration:** Human-centered AI and multi-agent systems that support controllable, adaptive, and seamless human–AI co-creation.

My work has been published at CHI, UIST, TheWebConf, IEEE TVCG, and IEEE VR, with Best Paper Honorable Mention recognitions at IEEE VR and ICSC. My research has also been supported by multiple research grants and has contributed to patents in the United States and China. More broadly, I aim to develop AI systems that make complex information intuitive while enabling people to maintain meaningful control over creative and intelligent systems.
